Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Keddy ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you interact with the Keddy WhatsApp AI Assistant (the "Service"), operated by Prince Ked Agbemenu.

1. Information We Collect

  • Phone number: Provided by WhatsApp/Twilio when you message us.
  • Message content: The text you send to the bot during your conversation.
  • Voice notes: Audio you send is transcribed to text using Groq Whisper to generate a reply.
  • Images: Photos you send are analyzed using Groq vision AI to describe content or answer your questions.
  • Session preferences: Language mode (formal or pidgin) and recent conversation history.
  • Technical logs: Timestamps and anonymized identifiers for service reliability.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate AI-powered responses to your messages.
  • To maintain conversation context within your current session.
  • To improve service reliability, security, and abuse prevention.
  • To honor opt-out and data deletion requests.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following providers to deliver the Service:

  • Meta / WhatsApp: Messaging platform.
  • Twilio: WhatsApp message delivery and webhook routing.
  • Groq: AI language model processing of your messages to generate replies.

Your message content is sent to Groq solely for the purpose of generating a response. Please review their privacy policies for details on how they handle data.

4. Data Retention

Conversation history is stored temporarily in server memory for the duration of your session (up to the last 6 message exchanges). Data may be cleared when the server restarts. We do not permanently store your messages in a database.

5. Your Rights and Choices

  • Opt out: Send STOP at any time to unsubscribe from the Service.
  • Re-subscribe: Send START to resume using the Service.
  • Delete data: Send DELETE to clear your conversation history from our session.
  • Help: Send HELP for usage information.

6. Security

We use HTTPS encryption, webhook signature validation, and input sanitization to protect data in transit and prevent abuse. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes.
